# Service Desk Policy: Ticket Priority, Routing and SLAs

## Priority Matrix
| Priority | Definition | Response SLA | Resolution SLA |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 – Critical | Production outage, security incident, or more than 10 users blocked | 15 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 – High | A single user fully blocked, or a business deadline at risk | 1 hour | 1 business day |
| P3 – Normal | Degraded service with a workaround, or a how-to question | 4 hours | 3 business days |
| P4 – Low | Service request (new software, access to shared drive) | 1 business day | 5 business days |

## Routing Rules
- Network/VPN/Wi-Fi → Network Operations
- Passwords, accounts, access rights → Identity & Access Management
- Outlook, Teams, SharePoint → Collaboration Services
- Laptops, monitors, docks, printers → End User Computing
- Phishing, malware, suspicious activity → Security Operations Centre (always P1)
- Payroll, leave, HR systems → HR Systems team

## Escalation
L1 must escalate to L2 if not resolved within 50% of the resolution SLA. Major incidents (P1 affecting more than 50 users) trigger the Major Incident Manager bridge call.

## Customer Communication
Every ticket gets an acknowledgement with the ticket number, priority, and next update time. Updates for P1 tickets are sent every 30 minutes.
