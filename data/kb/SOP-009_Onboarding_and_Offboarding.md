# SOP-009: Employee Onboarding and Offboarding (IT Tasks)

Owner: Service Desk with HR Systems

## Onboarding (triggered by HR 5 business days before the start date)
1. Account created in Active Directory / Entra ID with licences assigned by role.
2. Laptop imaged and enrolled in device management; delivered or handed over on day 1.
3. On day 1 the new joiner sets the password and registers MFA at the IT desk with photo ID.
4. Role-based group access is granted automatically; anything extra goes through SOP-008.

## Offboarding (triggered by HR on the last working day)
1. The account is disabled at 18:00 local time on the last working day; it is not deleted for 30 days.
2. The mailbox is converted to a shared mailbox and the manager is given access for 30 days.
3. The laptop and accessories must be returned to the IT desk; a remote wipe is issued if not returned within 5 business days.
4. Immediate termination: HR raises a P1 and the account is disabled within 1 hour.

## Contractors
Contractor accounts expire automatically on the contract end date and must be extended by the sponsoring manager.
