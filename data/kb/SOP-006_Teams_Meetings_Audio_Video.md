# SOP-006: Microsoft Teams Meetings, Audio and Video

Owner: Collaboration Services

## No Audio or Microphone in Teams
1. In Teams, open Settings → Devices and check that the correct speaker and microphone are selected. Docking stations often take over as the default device.
2. On Windows, check Privacy settings → Microphone → "Let desktop apps access your microphone" is On. On macOS, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → Microsoft Teams is enabled.
3. Make a test call (Settings → Devices → Make a test call).
4. Clear the Teams cache if problems persist: quit Teams, delete the contents of `%appdata%\Microsoft\Teams` (classic) or reset the app from Settings → Apps (new Teams), and restart.

## Camera Not Working
- Close other apps that may be using the camera (Zoom, browser tabs).
- Corporate laptops with a privacy shutter: make sure it is open.
- If the camera is missing in Device Manager, raise a hardware ticket for End User Computing.

## Meeting Room (Teams Rooms) Problems
Meeting room consoles are supported by the Workplace AV team, not the service desk. Re-route these tickets to Workplace AV with the room name. Target response is 15 minutes during office hours for rooms with a meeting in progress.

## Recording and Transcripts
Recordings are saved to the organiser's OneDrive and expire after 60 days unless the organiser changes the expiry date.
